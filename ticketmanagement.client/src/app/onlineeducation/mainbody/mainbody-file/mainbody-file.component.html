Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our e-Learning platform and exam preparation.

  • What is e.Learning & Technologies?

    e.Learning & Technologies is an online learning platform designed to help students prepare for competitive and academic examinations through structured study materials, practice questions, mock tests, and technology-based learning resources.

  • Which examinations are covered on the platform?

    We provide learning resources and practice materials for various competitive and academic examinations. The available courses, subjects, chapters, and tests may vary depending on the examination and syllabus.

  • What study materials are available?

    Students can access syllabus-wise and chapter-wise learning resources, practice questions, multiple-choice questions, mock tests, and performance-based test results to support their exam preparation.

  • Can I practice questions chapter by chapter?

    Yes. Our platform organizes learning content according to syllabus and chapters, allowing students to practice questions topic by topic and improve their understanding before attempting full-length tests.

  • Can I check my test performance and ranking?

    Yes. After completing a test, students can view their score, attempted questions, correct and incorrect answers, and other available performance information. Ranking features may also be provided for supported tests.
